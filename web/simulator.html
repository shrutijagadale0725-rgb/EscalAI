How it works

It's not a chatbot.
It's your case file.

Every complaint becomes a case with a letter, a deadline and a clear next step. It's saved, so you can come back to it.

2. Review and send

3. Track and escalate

Before you start

Keep it simple, keep it safe

We save your name, item, seller, order ID, amount, letters and case history so you can return to your case. Never enter card numbers, passwords or OTPs. This is not legal advice.
